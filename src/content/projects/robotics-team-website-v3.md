## Overview

This is the third version of the robotics team website. V1 was the bare minimum, V2 added navigation and polish, and V3 is the one that finally holds all the content the team actually wanted on it.

The jump from V2 was less about new tricks and more about structure. By this point I knew which sections the team kept asking for, so I could lay the page out around them instead of bolting things on afterwards.
