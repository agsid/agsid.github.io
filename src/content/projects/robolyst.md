## Overview

Robolyst is an operations suite built for robotics teams under the Robotics Catalyst Foundation. Teams juggle rosters, scouting notes, and season logistics across spreadsheets and group chats — Robolyst pulls that into one place.

## What it does

- Team rosters and role tracking across a season
- Scouting data entry and lookup during competitions
- Season planning tools shared across a team's mentors and students

## Tech stack

Built with **Svelte** for the frontend, a **Postgres** database for team and scouting data, and **Canva** integrations for quick asset generation (team graphics, printable scouting sheets).

## Status

Live and used by teams affiliated with the Robotics Catalyst Foundation. Ongoing work focuses on expanding scouting analytics and making onboarding faster for new teams.
