## Overview

SingulTech is the public-facing site for SingulTech Robotics — the front door for sponsors, parents, and prospective members to find out who the team is and what they're doing this season.

## What it does

- Team introduction and roster overview
- Sponsor recognition and sponsorship info
- Season updates and event announcements

## Tech stack

Built with **Svelte** on the frontend, a small **server** backend for content updates, and **Tailwind CSS** for styling.

## Status

Live and maintained alongside the team's season. Content gets refreshed each competition season.
