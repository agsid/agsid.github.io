## Overview
Made in partnership with FTC Team #7034sid

## What it does

- Hosted CTF challenges across common categories
- Account system so progress persists between sessions
- AI-assisted hints for players who get stuck, tuned to nudge rather than solve

## Tech stack

Built with **Svelte**, an **authentication** layer for accounts and progress tracking, and an **AI**-backed hint system.

## Status

Live at ctftc.robotics-catalyst.org, used as a practice ground ahead of live CTF competitions.
